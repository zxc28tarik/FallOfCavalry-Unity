# Historical Sources — Geography

Machine-readable provenance is committed at `UnityProject/Assets/FOC/Content/Resources/FOC/Geography/geography-sources.txt`; locations/routes refer to those stable source IDs.

The high-confidence Rumeli spine follows Sema Altunan's study of late-seventeenth-century İstanbul–Edirne menzils: İstanbul, Büyükçekmece, Silivri, Çorlu, Lüleburgaz and Edirne. Küçükçekmece is corroborated by Evliya Çelebi route material. The Anatolian spine follows the historically continuous Üsküdar–Gebze–İzmit road discussed by Enis Karakaya and Ottoman menzil scholarship. Suraiya Faroqhi and the İstanbul Encyclopedia evidence regular İstanbul–Mudanya movement and Evliya's Mudanya–Bursa journey.

Settlement existence and route sequence are historical claims. Map coordinates are modern approximate metadata for placement; segment distances and speeds are `SliceTuning`, not final historical measurements. The graph deliberately omits weakly sourced speculative shortcuts. Evliya is a contemporary witness but is cross-checked because individual descriptions can be rhetorical or exaggerated. Wikipedia was not used as sole authority.

Confidence values mean: `High` = route/location directly supported by multiple or focused academic/primary references; `Medium` = sound continuity evidence but not exact 1648 schedule; `Interpretative` = explicit design interpolation. No interpretative production route currently ships in this slice.
