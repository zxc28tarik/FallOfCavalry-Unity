Bounded presentation-contact proposal — NOT IMPLEMENTED
------------------------------------------------------
Prerequisite: original/calibrated target and, if needed, anatomically calibrated
source comparisons must first establish correct body/arm reference semantics.

* Build an immutable target-specific profile keyed by exact Avatar, mesh and
  clip hashes. At >=60 samples/second, evaluate the actual Animator and LOD0
  skin. Identify left/right heel, toe and sole samples from original Foot/Toes
  skin influences, rather than a whole-renderer AABB or a fixed ankle offset.
* Record per-foot sole height, foot/toe orientation and phase contact envelope.
  Validate stance masks against the motion. Preserve Running flight phases.
  Do not call a stationary local-XZ foot the only valid contact: an in-place
  walk naturally moves the planted foot backward relative to the actor.
* After animation evaluation, derive foot endpoint correction from the measured
  sole deficit to floor+0.002m. A small downward correction is allowed only in
  validated stance; positive collision protection may operate during swing.
  Solve two-bone leg IK using the current knee plane and original segment
  lengths. Initially preserve authored foot/toe rotation. Do not move campaign
  position, gameplay root or alter root-motion authority.
* Bound displacement by the measured leg length (initial proposal: 8%, around
  0.06m on this target), plus explicit knee-plane and extension bounds. If the
  correction cannot leave <=0.002m sole penetration without violating bounds,
  report failure; do not silently clamp and mark success. A fixed global +7cm
  lift is not this algorithm. Pelvis compensation, if needed for reach, requires
  a separately measured small bound and fresh two-foot validation.
* Re-bake held-out phases after correction. Check both soles, heel/toe hover,
  original stance XZ trajectory, knee behavior, loop seam and continuous phase
  interpolation. Use precomputed/contact-proxy data at runtime, not BakeMesh
  per actor per frame. Full skin baking belongs to authoring/validation.
