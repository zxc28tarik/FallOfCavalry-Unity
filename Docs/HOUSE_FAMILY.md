# Family, Household and House

Family, Household and House are separate facts. Family links are a deliberately limited set of important biological and marriage links, not a generated ancestry graph. Household is the Organization `Household` branch and can include family, servants, guards and close retinue; Household membership does not establish blood or House membership.

A House has a stable `HouseId`, immutable definition and persistent state. Capture, conquest, faction change, extinction or dissolution does not replace or hard-delete that identity. A living active House member may be head. An active House without a head must be explicitly succession-pending.

`PersonalWealth`, `HouseWealth` and `StateTreasuryBalance` are distinct types. House property uses a future-facing asset reference and category; this package does not create Estate, City or Trade asset gameplay.

Marriage is a limited social/family link and never automatically creates a faction alliance. Succession controls headship; inheritance controls private assets. PrivateProperty may name an heir. StateOffice and TimarDirlikServiceGrant cannot use ordinary automatic inheritance and become regrant-required instead. Full title, office and Timar allocation remain later-package work.
