CAUSE / ALGORITHM
The earlier audit_and_build_meshy_lods.py weld_derived_only() merges coincident
positions on the derived copy, then performs unconstrained collapse decimation.
It does not protect UV/material/normal/skin discontinuities or the waist/face.
The original LOD0 itself was not altered by that operation.

New meshy_lod2_constraints.py is a deterministic constrained edge-collapse
simplifier. Geometric connectivity may share coincident source positions, but
export retains distinct corner UV, normals and skin attributes. It locks:
- UV/material/hard-normal seams, boundaries and nonmanifold edges;
- true weight discontinuities at the SAME position across a seam;
- every original triangle touching the measured neck/head/headgear region;
- every original triangle touching the Hips-anchored waist/belt region;
- small disconnected source components, including source detail pieces.
Ordinary smoothly changing weights ALONG an edge do not constitute a seam.

Only unprotected manifold edges collapse, using endpoint/midpoint quadric cost,
the manifold link condition and a triangle normal/area safety check. A failed
reduction target does not unlock features. All protected feature triangles and
attributes are checked unchanged. Derived weights interpolate, then retain the
strongest four normalized influences; this is NOT an exact-source-weight claim
for reduced vertices. The source/runtime LOD0 rig/geometry/UV/weights/animations
remain byte- and independently snapshot-identical.

Target ratio defaults to .45 (roughly4314 triangles from9586). The request is a
guide, not permission to damage a protected feature. A safely higher count is
allowed only below the current5748-triangle LOD1. Existing2396-triangle LOD2 is
retained until a replacement is actually reviewed. A25mm bidirectional REST
surface-distance bound is a mechanical rejection threshold, NOT art acceptance.
Actual near/far silhouette, texture, belt, face and animated screenshots remain
mandatory; they may reject a candidate whose structure passes.
