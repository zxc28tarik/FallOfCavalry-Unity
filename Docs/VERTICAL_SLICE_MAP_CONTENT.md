# Vertical Slice Map Content

Production-candidate locations: İstanbul, Küçükçekmece, Büyükçekmece, Silivri, Çorlu, Lüleburgaz, Edirne, Üsküdar, Gebze, İzmit, Mudanya and Bursa. Routes form the Rumeli middle-road chain, the Bosphorus crossing plus Üsküdar–Gebze–İzmit road, and İstanbul–Mudanya sea plus Mudanya–Bursa land connection.

İstanbul is the selected capital and start. Its temporary City V2 bridge is `city-home`; Bursa temporarily bridges `city-other`. These are explicit 14A compatibility bridges, not final historical City content, and must be replaced in 14B without changing WorldLocation IDs.

The fictional player is `slice-player-sipahi`, display name `Hasan Ağa — SLICE_PLAYER`, and is marked `VERTICAL_SLICE_DEVELOPMENT`. This is a package-scoped playable start, not the universal campaign start.

The map shows public locations/routes, City navigation for linked locations, destination commands and known active-journey progress. Live actor journeys are shown only when `PresentationViewerContext` grants exact knowledge.
