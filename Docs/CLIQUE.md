# Clique and Internal Politics Foundation

A Clique is a social power network, not a state, House, Faction, Character, modern party or institution. The exact types are Merchant, Military, Religious, GuildCraft, UrbanNotables, PatronageHouseholdCircle and TribalNomadic. Ocak is an institution and is not a Clique type. Religious Clique describes a network only; no Religion/Sect identity or modifier exists here.

Membership is optional and a Character may join multiple Cliques. A leader is optional but, when present, must be an active member. The exposed hierarchy is limited to a parent and one subclique level.

Influence is calculated only from explicit member-backed source records. It is not an invented political resource. Clique state exposes no inventory, goods, soldiers, Army or military units and its combat bonus is always zero. Economic or military weight must eventually derive from real member/domain assets.

Attitude, pressure categories and `PoliticalReactionProjection` are future-facing derived outputs. Rebellion readiness requires both Power and Cause; it does not execute rebellion. Assassination, sabotage, secret agendas, covert networks and full political simulation are absent.
