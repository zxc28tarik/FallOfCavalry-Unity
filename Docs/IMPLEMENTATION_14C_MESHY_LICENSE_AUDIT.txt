The terms assign paid-plan Customer Output ownership to the customer as between
Meshy and that customer, to the extent the law permits. Incorporated service
assets (including rigs/animations) may be distributed only as needed to exploit
that output. Input rights remain the customer's responsibility. AI-identifying
metadata must not be removed. Meshy Community publication has separate terms;
we do not infer that this model was published there or label it CC0.

The official commercial-use article permits paid-model commercial use, resale
and delivery in client projects, subject to rights in underlying references.
It links ownership to the plan at creation and says paid attribution is optional.

Decision (inference from those sources, not an explicit GitHub-specific clause):
User confirmed creation in their own Meshy Pro account and authorized FOC use.
Commercial game integration and derivative optimization are supported by the
stated paid-output rights. Distribution of the optimized complete character with
its incorporated rig/motions is supported; isolated resale of Meshy service rigs
or animation-library assets is not assumed. No source-image rights certification
or paid-account history verification is claimed.

- Preserve AI/tool attribution, original hashes and relevant FBX metadata.
