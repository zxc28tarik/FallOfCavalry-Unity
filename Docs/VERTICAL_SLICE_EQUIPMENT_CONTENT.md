# Vertical-slice equipment content

Production definitions are `kilic` (sabre), `mizrak` (lance), `fitilli-tufek` (matchlock firearm), `zirh-gomlek` (body armor), `kalkan` (shield), `sipahi-ati` (mount) and `tufek-muhimmat` (ammunition). Persistent instances retain recruitment-record and economic-good provenance. Mounted-only/infantry-only restrictions, slots and ammunition-family compatibility remain executable invariants.

The Met Ottoman saber and early-seventeenth-century Ottoman helmet catalogue records support period/cultural existence; TDV Yeniçeri supports the firearm/ammunition institutional context. Exact attack capabilities, slot compatibility, quality code, inventory quantities and visual reuse are reconstruction or `SliceTuning`, not measurements copied from an artifact. The generated low-detail 3D modules are repository-authored procedural placeholders and remain non-final art.
