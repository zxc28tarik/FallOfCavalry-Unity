# Vertical-slice military content

`army-hasan-retinue` is a deliberately small fictional kapı halkı/retinue foundation, not an estimate of an attested formation. Its single finite recruitment source is `recruitment-hasan-retinue` (`Retinue`, 20 available), authorized through Hasan Ağa's Organization Army assignment. The Army has 12 aggregate personnel in three UnitGroups: four `troop-timarli-sipahi`, four `troop-cebeli` and four `troop-tufekli-piyade`.

Six named-by-ID persistent SoldierInstances represent a subset of the aggregate headcount: two sipahi, two cebeli and two firearm infantry. They preserve UnitGroup, recruitment record, loadout and equipment-instance identity through save/load. The Army carries real grain and ammunition supply and explicit requirements. Morale, fatigue, discipline, headcount and supply quantities are `SliceTuning`. 14B creates no battle or casualty state.
