# Religion Policy Foundation

Religion policy is represented separately from Religion identity and demographic profile. A typed City, Faction or Region target may define qualitative rules for a Religion or compatible Sect:

- recognition: unrecognized, recognized, official;
- treatment: tolerated, restricted, privileged;
- enforcement: nominal, local discretion, enforced.

These values are descriptive policy inputs. Implementation 3 assigns no numeric loyalty, unrest, relation, rebellion, war or combat effect to them. Enforcement and local variation are explicit data rather than hidden country-specific switches.
